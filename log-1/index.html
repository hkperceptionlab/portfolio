Log 1. Why I changed my first research idea

When I was in eleventh grade, my first idea was to study how student actors became immersed in their roles while preparing for a school play. I also thought about studying how people watching the play reacted to the actors and the stage.

However, to do this, I would have needed to collect experiences or comments from students at my school, and most of them were under 18. At that time, I did not know that this required informed consent and approval from my school. After I learned about these requirements, I did not feel comfortable using the experiences of other students on the theater team as research data without both, so I stopped the project.

After I returned to Korea, I watched Life of Pi. I became curious about what people remembered and how they described the performance. I decided to study public online reviews instead of asking students to participate directly.

This choice also changed what I could learn. I could not know everything people actually experienced while watching the play. I could study only what they chose to write in their reviews.
